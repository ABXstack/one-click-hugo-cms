## Support our work in British Columbia

Seniors for Climate in British Columbia is not a separately registered nonprofit. Donations that support our work are processed through the national Seniors for Climate organization.

We are currently working with the national organization on the best way to direct contributions to British Columbia. Details will be posted here once that is in place.

In the meantime, the most valuable thing you can give us is your time. [Join us](/take-action/join-us/) — we would be glad to have you.
