Climate action in British Columbia is busy, and it can be hard to keep track of. This page collects the places we go to find out what is actually going on — the networks, the event calendars, and the reports that hold our government to account.

If you are looking for something to attend, start with the event listings. If you want to know how BC is doing, start with the progress report.

## Networks and events

- [West Coast Climate Action Network (WE-CAN)](https://westcoastclimateaction.ca/we-can-news/) — Brings climate activists together, lobbies elected leaders, and runs webinars and working groups.
- [Events and webinars](https://creativelyunited.org/events/) — Creatively United — Event postings and webinars across BC. Anyone can [add an event to their calendar](https://creativelyunited.org/events/community/add).

## How BC is doing

### 2025 BC Climate Action Report

In January 2026, the BC Climate Emergency Campaign released its progress report on the ten urgent actions the province should be taking. The BC government received eight "FAIL" grades and two "Minor Progress" scores.

- [2025 Climate Progress Report](https://bcclimateemergency.ca/progress-report) — BC Climate Emergency Campaign — The full scorecard and the reasoning behind each grade.
- [CleanBC needs a renewal, not a retreat](https://vancouversun.com/opinion/op-ed/opinion-cleanbc-needs-a-renewal-not-a-retreat) — Vancouver Sun — An op-ed published in response to the report.
