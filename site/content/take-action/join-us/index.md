### There is room here for you.

However much time you have, and whatever you are good at.

## What this looks like

We are volunteer-run. Everything that happens here happens because someone decided to help.

You do not need a background in climate science or activism. Most of us didn't have one either. What we need is people willing to show up, learn alongside everyone else, and do one useful thing at a time.

## Ways to help

- **Building relationships.** Connecting with other seniors, community groups, and climate organizations across BC.
- **Education.** Helping people learn — sharing resources, hosting Climate Cafes, and passing along what you know.
- **Events.** Supporting the planning and running of events in communities across the province.
- **Meeting elected leaders.** Joining or helping organize meetings with MLAs and MPs.
- **Writing.** Letters to the editor, letters to elected officials, and helping others write theirs.

## Getting started

The simplest first step is to come to a meeting. We meet online once a month, and new people are always welcome — you are free to just listen the first time.

Email us at [bc-hub@seniorsforclimate.org](mailto:bc-hub@seniorsforclimate.org) and we will send you the details.
