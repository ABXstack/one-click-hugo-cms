**What will British Columbia look like during today's students' lifetimes?**

For young British Columbians, the next fifty years aren't an abstraction. They're their lives.

Resources accompanying *Under BC's Smoky Skies* will help teachers and students explore the science behind BC's changing climate, investigate the risks in their own communities, and talk about how societies prepare for an uncertain future.

The aim isn't to tell students what to think. It's to give them trustworthy information, real questions, and room to think hard about the province they're going to inherit.

## What students do

**Watch** — the documentary, and full speaker presentations.

**Understand** — background materials for teachers.

**Discuss** — questions built for the classroom.

**Investigate** — climate risks where they live.

**Imagine** — what a resilient BC could look like.

**Act** — age-appropriate community projects, spending choices, letters to elected representatives, and watching the film at home with family and friends.
