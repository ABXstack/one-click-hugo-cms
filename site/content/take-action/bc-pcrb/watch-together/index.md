**Some conversations are too important to watch alone.**

Picture six or eight friends in a living room. A seniors' group in a community hall. A family around the kitchen table. A class watching together.

Share some food, then talk.

We'll provide questions for before and after — the kind worth sitting with. What surprised you? What worries you? What gives you hope? What does this mean for your community? What do we owe the generations coming after us?

The Watch Together program is built to make hosting easy, anywhere in the province. You get the film, a simple host guide, discussion questions and resources for anyone who wants to go deeper.

Because the point of this project isn't to make something people watch. It's to start conversations that keep going after the screen goes dark.

## Host a home screening

1. Invite 6–12 people
2. Watch the film
3. Use the conversation guide and the before-and-after questions
4. Share food
5. Tell us what your group discussed

## Host a community screening

For libraries, seniors' centres, faith communities, environmental groups, service clubs, neighbourhood associations and anyone else with a room and an audience.
