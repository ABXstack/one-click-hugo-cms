Meeting your MP or MLA is one of the most effective things you can do, and one of the least mysterious once you have done it once. They are obliged to hear from constituents. Seniors, in particular, tend to get through the door.

The two resources below take you from the first request through to the meeting itself: how to organize a small group, what to prepare, and how to make your fifteen minutes count.
