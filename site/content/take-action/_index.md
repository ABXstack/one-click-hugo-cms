Everyone wants to move the needle, a little or a lot. This page is for turning that into something concrete.

We have split it in two. **Individual action** is what you can do from your kitchen table — write a letter, change a habit, sign on to a campaign. **Collective action** is what happens when we do it together, which is usually where the real leverage is. Pick whichever fits the week you are having.

Two things deserve their own page, and you will find them in the menu: [meeting with your elected officials](/take-action/meet-your-elected-officials/), which is one of the most effective things a senior can do, and [joining us](/take-action/join-us/), which is how most of this actually gets done.

## Individual action

### Write a letter to the editor

If the media won't make the connection between fossil fuels, climate change, and the wildfires we are living through, our letters can. Both toolkits below will help you get started.

- [How to publish a letter to the editor in your local newspaper](https://sierraclub.bc.ca/local-leaders/how-to-publish-a-letter-to-the-editor-in-your-local-newspaper/?mkt_tok=Nzg4LVlCRi0yOTYAAAGjkQv6deQZ7sAbuPuaEF0QflXJ9IAa24wH8WbWRycU6_am821OF_IKKu_ZZrqcOJuUhDai1qt7IccrLLslJFQSsnVn4bbXDlny-z_8WVNR) — Sierra Club BC — A step-by-step toolkit for getting a climate letter into your local paper.
- [Letter to the editor toolkit](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TR_bRIk4Pr0oUkuAncmmRVHyMytne0UarMGJyHNRwF4/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.vn4ozv38wsd2) — 350.org — Multiple sample letters you can adapt.

### Campaigns you can join

- [Protect Our Winters (POW) — Take Action](https://www.protectourwinters.ca/campaigns) — Founded by snowboarder Jeremy Jones and now active in 15 countries including Canada. Two days before the Milan-Cortina Winter Olympics, Norwegian skier and filmmaker Nikolai Schirmer hand-delivered a "Ski Fossil Free" petition with more than 21,000 signatures to the IOC's head of sustainability, calling for a report on the ethical, environmental, and health implications of fossil fuel sponsorship in winter sports.
- [Who Sucks](https://whosucks.ca/) — Code Blue BC — A campaign about industrial use and abuse of BC's fresh water. The largest and most profitable corporations in the province pay a fraction of a penny per litre for billions of litres of water.
  - [A short (and funny) video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFcEIyIP958) — YouTube — The quickest way to understand the campaign.
  - [Letterwriter tool](https://www.codebluebc.ca/letterwriter) — Code Blue BC — Write to the BC government in a few minutes.
  - [Modernizing Industrial Water Rates in BC, October 2025](https://watershedsecurity.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/REPORT-Modernizing-Industrial-Water-Rates-in-BC-Oct-2025-1.pdf) — Watershed Security (PDF) — The report behind the numbers.

## Collective action

### Seniors' Climate Witness

Good ancestors don't build pipelines. A group from Victoria is planning a low-carbon caravan from the west coast to Ottawa, stopping along the way and picking up other witnesses as it goes. They invite every community to take part in whatever way feels right.

Seniors, people of faith, and allies are gathering in Ottawa for a week of nonviolent action calling on the federal government to stop fossil fuel expansion and protect a livable future — **September 19–26, 2026**.

- [Sign up to join](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSegagmChsIpTh8dV16IjfnoC_VYs-s1zFOLvyrcoXcp3FdbIQ/viewform) — Seniors' Climate Witness — The registration form for the week in Ottawa.
- Questions? Email [seniors.climate.witness@proton.me](mailto:seniors.climate.witness@proton.me)
