The last decade has changed what is possible. Solutions that were theoretical are now being built, costed, and measured — including here in British Columbia.

This page is about what is working. Some of it is provincial research, some of it is your own municipality's climate plan, and some of it is the global picture. It is a good antidote to the feeling that nothing is being done.
