Most of us know the broad outline: emissions have been climbing since the Industrial Revolution. But the details matter, and they have a way of getting muddled — especially when misinformation is circulating.

These are the explanations we return to. Some are quick reads, some are video series you can work through over a few evenings. All of them come from sources that show their evidence.

## Resources

- [What is climate change?](https://davidsuzuki.org/what-you-can-do/what-is-climate-change/) — David Suzuki Foundation — A thorough overview of climate change and of the two ways we respond to it: mitigation and adaptation.
- [Weathered video series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M4pSD1D1JMo) — YouTube — A current, well-made video series on what climate change is and how we got here.
- [Causes and Effects of Climate Change](https://www.un.org/en/climatechange/science/causes-effects-climate-change) — United Nations — A clear, plain-language summary of the science.
- [British National Emergency Briefing — Climate Change](https://www.patreon.com/posts/complete-change-144741531) — A 25-minute video summary of the briefing delivered to the British Government on the scale of the climate emergency.
- [British National Emergency Briefing — full series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lpLxLp4j07Y&list=PL6b0HBZ3v-IkuFdfsieHeQVvo2rDWoADE&index=1) — YouTube — The long version: ten presenters, ten videos, five to fifteen minutes each.
- [More about the National Emergency Briefing](https://www.wedonthavetime.org/events/national-emergency-briefing) — We Don't Have Time — Background on the briefing and the people behind it.
- [Climate Change Articles](http://snopes.com) — Snopes — A reliable place to check and debunk common climate misinformation.
