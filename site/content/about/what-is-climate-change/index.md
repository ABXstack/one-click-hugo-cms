Most of us know the broad outline: emissions have been climbing since the Industrial Revolution. But the details matter, and they have a way of getting muddled — especially when misinformation is circulating.

These are the explanations we return to. Some are quick reads, some are video series you can work through over a few evenings. All of them come from sources that show their evidence.
