Do Canadians care about the climate? They sure do.

One of the most persistent myths about climate action is that most people have stopped caring. The research says otherwise — and it says so consistently, here in British Columbia and across the country. The polls, reports, and videos below are worth knowing about, because they are useful ammunition in a conversation with a neighbour, a letter to the editor, or a meeting with an elected official.
