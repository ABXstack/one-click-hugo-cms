Do Canadians care about the climate? They sure do.

One of the most persistent myths about climate action is that most people have stopped caring. The research says otherwise — and it says so consistently, here in British Columbia and across the country. The polls, reports, and videos below are worth knowing about, because they are useful ammunition in a conversation with a neighbour, a letter to the editor, or a meeting with an elected official.

## Re.Climate

- [Canadians Haven't Given Up on Climate Action. They Just Think Everyone Else Has.](https://reclimate.ca/canadians-havent-given-up-on-climate-action-they-just-think-everyone-else-has/) — Re.Climate — Hopeful research on the gap between what Canadians actually believe and what they assume everyone else believes. Includes Canadian videos and a resource library.
- [Canadians (Still) Care About Climate Change](https://reclimate.ca/resource/canadians-still-care-about-climate-change-technical-report/) — Re.Climate — Two short videos: one on Canada as a renewable energy superpower, one on how much Canadians still care.
- [2026 Technical Report](https://reclimate.ca/canadians-still-care-about-climate-change-technical-report/) — Re.Climate — The full data behind the findings above.
- [Re.Climate resource library](https://reclimate.ca/resource-library/) — Re.Climate — A broad collection of Canadian climate communication research to browse.

## National and global

- [Climate concern remains high, but affordability still dominates Canadians' priorities](https://abacusdata.ca/climate-concern-remains-high-but-affordability-still-dominates-canadians-priorities/) — Abacus Data, May 2026 — Concern about climate is holding steady, but cost of living is what people rank first.
- [89Percent](https://89percent.org/) — Between 80 and 89 percent of people worldwide want their governments doing more about climate change.
