We mobilize seniors in British Columbia for climate action advocacy.

We do that three ways: by building relationships, by helping people learn, and by supporting the groups and events that make action happen across the province. We create community. And we meet with our elected leaders.

We are facing an intergenerational climate emergency. We need faster and better climate action now — for our children, our grandchildren, and everyone who comes after them.

**Later is too late!**

## Why seniors

We have time, we have experience, and we have credibility with the people who make decisions. We also have the longest view of what we are handing on.

Climate change is already harder on older bodies — heat, wildfire smoke, and flooding do more damage to us than to most. That gives us a stake in this twice over: as people at risk, and as people with the standing to speak up.
