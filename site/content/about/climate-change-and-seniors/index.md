---
title: "Climate Change and Seniors"
description: "Why older adults are especially at risk from climate change — and the role seniors can play in the response."
---

Climate change reaches everyone, but it does not reach everyone equally. Heat waves, wildfire smoke, and flooding are harder on older bodies, and existing health conditions make the risk sharper still.

That is one reason seniors have a stake in this. The other is that we have time, experience, credibility with decision-makers, and a long view of what we are handing on. The resources here cover both sides: how to protect yourself and the people around you, and why our generation's voice matters in this fight.
